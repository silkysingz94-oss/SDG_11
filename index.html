Methodology & interpretation

Land consumption rate (LCR):
[ln(Urb₂/Urb₁) / number of years] × 100.

Population growth rate (PGR):
[ln(Pop₂/Pop₁) / number of years] × 100.

SDG 11.3.1:
LCR ÷ PGR.

A ratio above 1 indicates that land consumption is increasing faster than population growth.
The population and land-cover periods should be temporally comparable. Confirm the 2013 population field and built-up class before reporting an official indicator.
