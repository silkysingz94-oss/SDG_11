10 · SDG 11.3.1 methodology

Land consumption rate (LCR)

LCR = ln(Urb / Ura) / y × 100


Population growth rate (PGR)

PGR = ln(Prb / Pra) / y × 100


SDG 11.3.1

Ratio = LCR / PGR


A ratio above 1 indicates that land consumption is increasing faster than population. A ratio below 1 indicates that population is increasing faster than land consumption.


These calculations must use comparable geographical boundaries, dates and population definitions. A national estimate should not automatically be reported as a city-level official SDG estimate.
