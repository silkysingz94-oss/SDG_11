Methodology
Land Consumption Rate (LCR)
LCR = [ln(Urb₂ / Urb₁) / n] × 100

Population Growth Rate (PGR)
PGR = [ln(Pop₂ / Pop₁) / n] × 100

SDG Indicator 11.3.1
SDG 11.3.1 = LCR / PGR

Urb₁ and Urb₂ are built-up areas. Pop₁ and Pop₂ are population totals. n is the observation period in years.
