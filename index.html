15 · Methodology

Land consumption rate

LCR = ln(Urb / Ura) / y × 100


Population growth rate

PGR = ln(Prb / Pra) / y × 100


SDG indicator 11.3.1

Ratio = LCR / PGR


A ratio greater than 1 means land consumption is growing faster than population. A ratio below 1 means population is growing faster than land consumption.


Built-up area is used as the land-consumption proxy in this dashboard. Official SDG reporting requires a consistent definition of urban extent and population.
